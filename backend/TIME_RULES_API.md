Notes
- `activate_days` and `deactivate_days` are lists of day numbers 0–6 (0=Sunday). They are converted to a cron-style string internally.
- `start_hour` and `stop_hour` accept integers 0–23 (numeric strings allowed). Minutes are fixed at 0.
